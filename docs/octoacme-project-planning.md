# OctoAcme — Project Planning

## Purpose
Turn an approved initiative into an actionable plan and backlog for delivery.

## Objectives
- Break work into shippable increments
- Identify dependencies and risks
- Align timelines, releases, and responsibilities
- Define clear roles and decision authority

## Activities
1. **Project Kickoff** with stakeholders and delivery team
   - *Led by*: Project Manager
   - *Participants*: Product Manager, Product Owner, Developers, QA Lead, Risk Owner, Communications Lead, Release Manager
   - *Outcomes*: Shared vision, goals, timeline, and role clarity

2. **Create prioritized backlog** with acceptance criteria
   - *Led by*: Product Manager and Product Owner
   - *Participants*: Developers, QA Lead
   - *Outcomes*: Prioritized backlog with clear acceptance criteria

3. **Estimate scope** (T-shirt sizing or story points)
   - *Led by*: Product Owner
   - *Participants*: Developers, QA Lead
   - *Outcomes*: Estimated backlog items with confidence levels

4. **Define Definition of Done (DoD)**
   - *Led by*: QA Lead and Developers
   - *Participants*: Product Owner, Product Manager
   - *Outcomes*: Agreed quality standards and completion criteria

5. **Identify dependencies and integration points**
   - *Led by*: Project Manager and Risk Owner
   - *Participants*: Developers, Product Owner
   - *Outcomes*: Dependency map and integration plan

6. **Create release plan and milestone map**
   - *Led by*: Release Manager and Project Manager
   - *Participants*: Product Manager, Product Owner, Risk Owner
   - *Outcomes*: Release schedule and quality gates

7. **Establish communication strategy**
   - *Led by*: Communications Lead
   - *Participants*: Project Manager, Product Manager
   - *Outcomes*: Stakeholder communication plan and templates

## Backlog Item Template
- **Title**: Clear, descriptive title
- **Description**: Problem statement and context
- **Acceptance Criteria**: Specific, testable criteria for completion
- **Priority**: Priority relative to other items
- **Estimate**: T-shirt size (XS/S/M/L/XL) or story points
- **Owner**: Primarily responsible developer(s)
- **Related Docs/Links**: Reference materials and dependencies
- **Quality Considerations**: Testing approach, edge cases
- **Risk Assessment**: Known risks and mitigation

## Sprint / Iteration Planning
- **Timebox planning** to agreed sprint length
- **Pull items** that meet DoD and have clear acceptance criteria
- **Assign Product Owner** to each item for clarification
- **Ensure team capacity** is respected and realistic
- **Identify blockers and dependencies** with Risk Owner
- **Review quality approach** with QA Lead

## Risk & Dependency Management
- **Capture in Risk Register**:
  - ID, Description, Impact, Probability, Owner (Risk Owner), Mitigation
- **Mark cross-team dependencies** in the project board
- **Escalate during weekly syncs** with Risk Owner and Project Manager
- **Define mitigation owners** with clear accountability
- **Monitor progress** against mitigation plans

## Role-Specific Responsibilities During Planning

### Product Manager
- Define business goals and success metrics
- Prioritize backlog
- Align with stakeholders on strategy

### Product Owner
- Clarify requirements and user stories
- Define acceptance criteria
- Confirm understanding with team

### Project Manager
- Coordinate planning activities
- Create project timeline and milestones
- Manage cross-team dependencies

### Developers
- Estimate effort required
- Identify technical risks and dependencies
- Propose technical approach

### QA Lead
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Create test strategy and approach
- Identify testing-related dependencies

### Risk Owner
- Identify project risks early
- Define mitigation strategies
- Establish escalation paths

### Release Manager
- Define release schedule and approach
- Identify deployment dependencies
- Plan release quality gates

### Communications Lead
- Define stakeholder communication plan
- Create status report templates
- Plan announcement strategy

## Planning Checklist
- [ ] Project kickoff held with all key roles
- [ ] Backlog prioritized and estimated
- [ ] Acceptance criteria defined for all items
- [ ] Definition of Done documented and agreed
- [ ] Release timeline and milestones agreed
- [ ] Initial test plan / QA approach drafted
- [ ] Dependencies and risks identified
- [ ] Escalation paths and decision authority clarified
- [ ] Communication strategy and cadence defined
- [ ] Role responsibilities confirmed with team
- [ ] Quality gates for release defined
- [ ] Improvement tracking plan established
